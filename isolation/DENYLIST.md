# Blind-phase denylist

Until the blind seal, do not fetch or inspect:

- the supplementary `Archive.zip` attached to the White Rose thesis record;
- the IISc FreeRTOS verification artifact referenced by the paper;
- VCC-annotated FreeRTOS source;
- Z/Eves or ProZ models/scripts;
- any original abstraction map, invariant file, ghost model, proof script, or
  generated verification condition;
- mirrors, snippets, search results, or AI answers that reproduce those
  materials;
- repository tree listings for the original proof artifact.

Forbidden material must not be placed in this Git repository, even encrypted
or ignored.  If accidental exposure occurs, stop, record the exact material
and affected questions in `CONTAMINATION_LEDGER.md`, and decide whether to
restart from a clean investigator/context before continuing.
