# License notes

The official V6.1.1 archive states that the FreeRTOS source is under GPL v2
plus the historical FreeRTOS exception.  The verbatim upstream license is kept
at `upstream/FreeRTOSV6.1.1/License/license.txt`.

The exception contains a historical restriction on competitive/comparative
use, including publication of FreeRTOS run-time or compile-time metrics without
permission.  This experiment does not benchmark scheduler performance and does
not publish FreeRTOS run-time or C compile-time comparisons.  Proof-development
and Isabelle checker timings are internal research-cost evidence, kept distinct
from product performance claims.  Any external publication should receive a
fresh license review rather than rely on this note.

All copied upstream files remain unmodified in `upstream/`.  Any proof-port or
bug-fix revision is stored separately with a prominent change ledger.
